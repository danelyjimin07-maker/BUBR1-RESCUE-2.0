# Methods and decision framework

## 1. Genotype review
The challenge WGS was reviewed using targeted, privacy-preserving extraction around MVA-relevant genes. Two BUB1B variants were prioritized: p.Leu737Ter and N1002K. N1002K was independently annotated with Ensembl VEP and reviewed against population and computational evidence. Phase is unresolved.

## 2. Mechanism classification
The proposed experiment first determines whether N1002K behaves as:
- an abundance/stability defect;
- an intrinsic functional defect; or
- a functionally neutral/non-causal variant.

Published BUBR1 MVA alleles are used as mechanistic precedent, not as proof of N1002K behavior.

## 3. Candidate selection
Approved drugs are matched to molecular bottlenecks rather than to the disease name alone. Arimoclomol tests proteostasis rescue; nicotinic acid tests an orthogonal NAD+/SIRT2-linked stability route; 4-PBA is a proteostasis comparator.

## 4. Experimental sequence
1. Baseline BUB1B mRNA and full-length BUBR1 protein.
2. Drug-specific target engagement.
3. Full-length BUBR1 abundance and localization.
4. Spindle assembly checkpoint function.
5. Chromosome segregation / micronuclei or related aneuploidy endpoints.
6. Safety / inappropriate proliferative-fitness gate.

## 5. Go/No-Go
GO requires concordant target engagement, molecular rescue, functional rescue, improved chromosome-segregation phenotype, and acceptable safety. Any broken link is a No-Go for that mechanism.

## AI assistance disclosure
OpenAI ChatGPT, Plus plan, was used for literature organization, hypothesis structuring, drafting, code/document preparation, and interpretation support. The account-level "Improve the model for everyone" setting was enabled during the analysis. Raw FASTQ files and the complete genome-wide VCF were not uploaded to ChatGPT; genomic analysis was performed locally, and only targeted, non-reconstructive variant summaries and selected annotation outputs were provided for assistance. AI-generated outputs were treated as supportive material and were independently checked against primary literature, public variant databases, and the challenge data before inclusion in the final interpretation.
