# Structural analysis of BUBR1 p.Asn1002Lys

## Objective

To investigate whether the BUBR1 p.Asn1002Lys (N1002K) variant occurs in a structurally constrained region that could plausibly affect BUBR1 folding, stability, or function.

## Structural model

The AlphaFold model of human BUBR1 (UniProt O60566; 1050 amino acids) was used for local structural inspection.

Residue N1002 lies within the C-terminal BUBR1 pseudokinase domain.

The local AlphaFold confidence for N1002 is high:

- N1002 pLDDT: ~91
- T1008 pLDDT: ~92
- L1012 pLDDT: ~95

Because this is a computationally predicted structure rather than an experimentally determined structure, all structural observations are interpreted as hypothesis-generating evidence.

## Local structural environment

Atoms within approximately 6 Å of N1002 include residues such as:

- W978
- V998
- N1004
- I1000
- F977
- R999
- W973
- F997
- K979
- S976
- F954
- E1006

The predicted N1002 side chain forms close geometric contacts with the surrounding structure.

Two particularly close predicted contacts are approximately:

- N1002 OD1 – W978 N: ~2.8 Å
- N1002 ND2 – V998 O: ~2.8 Å

These distances are compatible with local polar interactions in the predicted model, although they do not constitute experimental evidence of hydrogen bonding.

## Physicochemical consequence of N1002K

The variant replaces:

Asparagine (N)
- polar
- uncharged
- relatively compact side chain

with:

Lysine (K)
- positively charged
- longer side chain
- different hydrogen-bonding and electrostatic properties

Therefore, N1002K introduces a substantial local physicochemical change within a structurally ordered region of the BUBR1 pseudokinase domain.

This raises the testable hypothesis that N1002K could perturb local packing, folding, protein stability, or domain function.

## Relationship to T1008 and L1012

N1002 is close to T1008 and L1012 in primary sequence.

However, analysis of the AlphaFold model does not support a direct structural contact between N1002 and either residue.

Approximate distances:

- N1002–T1008 minimum atomic distance: ~9.1 Å
- N1002–L1012 minimum atomic distance: ~10.4 Å

Therefore, no direct N1002–T1008 or N1002–L1012 interaction is claimed.

## Interpretation

The structural analysis strengthens the rationale for experimentally testing N1002K but does not establish pathogenicity.

The combined evidence currently supporting functional follow-up includes:

1. Extremely low population frequency.
2. Absence of homozygotes in gnomAD.
3. Strong AlphaMissense prioritization.
4. Localization within the BUBR1 pseudokinase domain.
5. High-confidence predicted local structure.
6. A substantial physicochemical change from Asn to Lys.
7. A compact predicted interaction environment around N1002.

## Testable predictions

If N1002K causes a protein-stability defect, cells expressing N1002K may show:

- reduced BUBR1 abundance;
- increased BUBR1 turnover;
- altered mitotic BUBR1 behavior;
- impaired chromosome alignment or spindle checkpoint function.

A rescue strategy should therefore not be considered successful solely because BUBR1 abundance increases.

Functional restoration should also be demonstrated.

## Claim discipline

This analysis does NOT demonstrate that N1002K:

- is pathogenic;
- destabilizes BUBR1;
- disrupts T1008 phosphorylation;
- interacts directly with L1012;
- causes MVA;
- responds to arimoclomol or 4-PBA.

These remain experimentally testable hypotheses.
